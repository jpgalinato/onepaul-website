GUIDE / TEAM PLANNING

Five questions before you hire offshore

What will the person own? Who will manage their work? Which tools will they use? What does success look like in 30, 60 and 90 days? How will you support their onboarding?

Clear responsibilities, timely feedback and thoughtful onboarding give dedicated professionals the best chance of becoming a lasting part of your business.

GUIDE / COSTS

Understand the fully loaded cost

Salary is only part of a staffing budget. Employer contributions, benefits, allowances, equipment and service fees can change the total. Compare itemised quotes rather than salary alone.
