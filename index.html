GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers.

Can we start with just one person?

Yes. You can begin with a single dedicated role and add people as your needs evolve.

Who manages the employee's work?

Your business directs the day-to-day responsibilities and priorities. Onepaul supports the agreed local employment arrangements.

Can people work Australian hours?

We’ll discuss your preferred working hours when defining the role. Availability and any additional costs are agreed before hiring.

What does fully loaded cost mean?

Salary plus applicable employer contributions, benefits and other agreed employment expenses. These are itemised separately from the service fee.
